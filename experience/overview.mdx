My career has followed a single thread: join organizations doing something genuinely hard, figure out what people infrastructure they need to scale without breaking, and build it. That's meant different things at different companies — designing compensation philosophy from first principles at Google's pre-IPO Alphabet bets, standing up a data warehouse and automating half the HR stack during OpenAI's ChatGPT moment, and now rebuilding performance, comp, and payroll workflows at Tools for Humanity using AI tooling I write myself. The throughline isn't any single functional area. It's the orientation: treat every people problem as a systems problem, build for scale rather than for today, and hold the bar on craft even when things are moving fast.

## Career Timeline

<Steps>
  <Step title="Google — Compensation Foundations Across Alphabet (2016–2022)">
    Six years at Google, progressing from People Ops Coordinator to Compensation Partner advising Alphabet's autonomous pre-IPO companies. Built deep expertise in compensation philosophy, survey benchmarking (Croner, Mercer, Radford GTS), job architecture, and cyclical comp planning — running programs across six companies simultaneously. Developed a reputation for technical fluency by coaching partners across Google and the Bets on SQL, reducing reliance on spreadsheet-only workflows. The foundation years as a Compensation Design Analyst and People Ops Coordinator established the operational rigor that everything since has been built on.
  </Step>
  <Step title="OpenAI — Scaling People Ops Through Exponential Growth (2022–2025)">
    Joined OpenAI in August 2022 — three months before ChatGPT launched and the company entered a period of historic growth. Progressed from Compensation, Benefits, and Operations Manager to Director of HR Operations, Systems, and Analytics. Led the Workday implementation from business case through go-live, rebuilt incentive design from first principles, automated manual data movement across systems via APIs, and built the company's people data warehouse with ETL pipelines and role-based dashboards. Managed a team spanning systems, benefits, operations, and global expansion while cultivating a culture of autonomy and technical depth.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Tools for Humanity — People Ops, Systems, and Rewards (2025–Present)">
    Hired as People Operations, Systems, and Rewards Manager at Tools for Humanity — the company behind World App and Worldcoin's biometric identity network. Leading a second Workday implementation, rebuilding performance and compensation programs to emphasize evidence-based feedback, automating payroll and equity workflows, and providing HRBP coverage including investigations and involuntary exits. Writing production automations using Claude Code and Codex to handle award communications, letter distribution, and payroll reconciliation — embedding AI directly into people operations infrastructure.
  </Step>
</Steps>

## Meta-Themes Across All Roles

  </Card>

What connects these roles isn't a company type or a stage — it's that each one required building something from low infrastructure in a high-stakes environment, and doing it with small teams who needed to punch above their weight. That's the work I find most interesting, and it's what I'd bring to Mintlify.
