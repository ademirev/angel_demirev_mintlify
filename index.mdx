## The Short Version

Angel has built the People infrastructure at two of the world's leading AI companies during their most critical growth phases. At OpenAI, he managed the scaling transition from ~50 to 150+ employees, building the data warehouse, automating critical workflows, and redesigning compensation from first principles. At Tools for Humanity, he led a Workday implementation, rebuilt performance and compensation programs, and built automations using Claude Code and Codex.

He brings deep technical fluency — he doesn't just partner with engineering, he builds alongside them. He brings executive-level compensation expertise, having designed benchmarking, pay bands, equity refresh strategies, and job architecture at companies where the stakes of getting this wrong are very high.

Most importantly, he has lived every inflection point Mintlify is about to hit.
